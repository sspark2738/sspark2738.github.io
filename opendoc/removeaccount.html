Account and Data Deletion Request

This page outlines the procedure for requesting the deletion of your account and associated personal data for games and applications (collectively, the "Services") provided by Smilecookie Inc.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

If you are unable to delete your account directly through the in-app settings menu, or if you prefer to submit a deletion request via the web, please contact our support team at the email address below. Your request will be processed promptly upon identity verification.

Support Email: support@smilecookie.com
* To ensure smooth processing, please include the [Name of the Game] and your [User ID or Linked Email Address] in the body of your email.

2. Data Deleted Upon Request

Once your account deletion request is completed, the following data will be permanently erased in an unrecoverable manner:

  • Account identification details (linked platform IDs, email address, nickname, etc.)
  • Gameplay progression data (stage records, in-game currency, items, character data, etc.)
  • Any other user preferences and logs generated during the use of the Services

3. Data Retention Policy

In principle, all personal data is destroyed without delay upon completion of your deletion request. However, where retention is required by applicable laws and regulations (such as e-commerce and consumer protection laws), limited transaction records may be securely retained for the statutory period before permanent deletion:

  • Records of contracts or subscription withdrawals (in-app purchases): 5 years
  • Records of consumer complaints or dispute resolution: 3 years
Important Notice: Once your account is deleted, all purchased and free in-game currencies, items, and gameplay progress will be permanently lost and cannot be restored under any circumstances. Requests are processed within 7 business days of receipt.
